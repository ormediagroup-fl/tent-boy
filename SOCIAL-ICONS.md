# Social icon sources

Social brand silhouettes (except LinkedIn) are bundled from the Simple Icons project, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0-1.0), retrieved November 5, 2026. Brand marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. LinkedIn uses a locally drawn monochrome mark. Icons are decorative alongside accessible text labels and link only to profiles configured by the site owner. No third-party scripts or icon CDN requests are used.

The Tent Boy tent/aperture mark is an original SVG created for this project. The editable mark is assets/tent-boy-mark.svg; the favicon uses the same drawing.
